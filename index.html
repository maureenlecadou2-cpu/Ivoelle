The Maison

An Ivorian house, a global voice

IVOELLE speaks the language of Côte d'Ivoire — Baoulé masks, Senufo geometry, the cowrie. Every design is original, never a borrowed print.

Based in the United States and shipping worldwide, IVOELLE dresses a global clientele who wear heritage as couture.

We begin with silk. In time, the maison will grow — a house of African luxury, refined for the world.
